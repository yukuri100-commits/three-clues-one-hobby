How to play

  1. Look at the first picture. Think of an activity.
  2. Talk to your partner. Reveal more clues when you need them.
  3. Type your guess, or say it aloud. Reveal the answer and read the full phrase.

Activity sets

Choose Core 8, Extra 10 or All 18 on the welcome slide. Spelling and partner talk use the same selected set. A new set starts a fresh lesson.

A short classroom session

Selected picture mysteries6 minutes
Read the phrases and recall them4 minutes
Spelling challenge2 minutes
Partner speaking3 minutes

Everyone thinks before anyone answers. Accept a reasonable idea, then use the final picture to introduce the target phrase.

Presentation controls

Space reveals the next clue or answer. Left and right arrow keys change slides. F toggles full screen. Use Slides to jump to any activity. Your work stays while this file remains open.

The Listen button uses an English voice available on your device. If no local English voice is available, read the phrase together.

All 54 illustrations are embedded in this file. Open it in a browser to use the activities without an internet connection.
